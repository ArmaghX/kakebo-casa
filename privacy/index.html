Privacy Policy

Kakebo Casa is a private personal budgeting application used exclusively by members of one household.

The application processes bank account transaction information obtained with the explicit consent of the account holder for the purpose of personal budgeting, expense categorisation and savings tracking.

Financial information is not sold or shared with third parties, except for service providers technically required to operate the application.

Bank access may be revoked by the account holder at any time.
